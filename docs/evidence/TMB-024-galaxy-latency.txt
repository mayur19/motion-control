The gyro detects rotation the camera cannot see, so the stimulus flash appears
before, or 2 frames after, the first VISIBLE marker motion:
  flash at frame 779:  phone visibly still 770-778; edge motion visible ~784
                       (TMB-024-galaxy-flash-before-visible-motion-1.jpg)
  flash at frame 8684: first visible shift 8682, i.e. 2 frames (8 ms) earlier;

A visible-onset rule therefore yields only a lower bound (<= 0-8 ms), not the
latency. The frame-assist tool's first pass also confirmed that the flash's
room illumination registers as marker "motion" when the marker region
overlaps lit surfaces.

Method change required for a valid camera measurement
-----------------------------------------------------
Hold the phone screen-on, facing the camera, and rotate it in the image plane
(like a steering wheel) so the first degrees of rotation move a high-contrast
corner laterally; place the marker region away from surfaces the flash lights.
